# ♞ Chess
<!-- yeraz {"color":{"heading":"green"}} -->

A two-player strategy game on an 8×8 board. It descends from *chaturanga* (India, about the 6th century) and reached its modern rules around 1475 in Europe, when the queen became the strongest piece.

## Phases of a game

- [Openings](/Chess/openings.md): development and theory.
- Middlegame: plans, tactics and prophylaxis.
- [Endgames](/Chess/endgames.md): technique, and now tablebases.
