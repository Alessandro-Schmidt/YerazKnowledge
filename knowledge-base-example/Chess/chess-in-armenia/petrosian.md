# 🛡️ Tigran Petrosian

Tigran Petrosian (1929–1984), the 9th World Champion. He was born in Tbilisi to Armenian parents and won the title from Botvinnik in 1963. He defended it against Spassky in 1966 and lost it to him in 1969.

## "Iron Tigran"

He was the master of **prophylaxis**: stop the opponent's plan before it starts. He was famously hard to beat, and he made the exchange sacrifice a defensive weapon. His openings → [Openings](/Chess/openings.md).

He became a national hero. Yerevan's main chess centre bears his name → [Chess in Armenia](/Chess/chess-in-armenia.md).
