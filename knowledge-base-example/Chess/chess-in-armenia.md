# 🏆 Chess in Armenia
<!-- yeraz {"color":{"heading":"amber"}} -->

Where all three subjects meet: a Christian nation with an old mathematical tradition, which made a game of logic a matter of state.

## Chess in schools

In **2011** Armenia became the first country to make chess compulsory in primary schools (grades 2–4). The stated aim is to train logic and decision-making, the skills of [Mathematics](/Mathematics/mathematics.md) and [Game theory](/Mathematics/game-theory.md).

## Players

- [Tigran Petrosian](/Chess/chess-in-armenia/petrosian.md): World Champion 1963–1969.
- **Levon Aronian**: peak rating 2830 (2014), world No. 2 → [Probability](/Mathematics/probability.md) for how that number is computed.
- Rafael Vaganian, Vladimir Akopian, Smbat Lputian: the generation that won the Olympiads.

## Team results

Gold at the Chess Olympiad in **2006** (Turin), **2008** (Dresden) and **2012** (Istanbul). Also World Team Champions in 2011.
