# 🔢 Number theory

The properties of the integers: primes, divisibility, congruences. Gauss called it the "queen of mathematics".

## The wheat and the chessboard

According to the legend, the inventor of chess asked for one grain of wheat on the first square, two on the second, and so on, doubling each time. The total is a geometric series:

1 + 2 + 4 + … + 2⁶³ = **2⁶⁴ − 1 = 18,446,744,073,709,551,615**

That is far more wheat than has ever been harvested. 2⁶⁴ − 1 is also the largest 64-bit integer. Chess engines store a board as 64-bit "bitboards", one bit per square → [Chess engines](/Chess/engines.md).
