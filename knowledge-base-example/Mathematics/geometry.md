# 📏 Geometry

The study of shape, size and space, from Euclid's axioms (c. 300 BC) to curved spaces and topology.

## The chessboard as a metric space

Every piece measures distance its own way:

- **King**: Chebyshev distance, max(|Δx|, |Δy|). A king crosses the board diagonally in 7 moves, not 14.
- **Rook**: any square is reachable in at most 2 moves.
- **Bishop**: stays on one colour, half the board, forever.
- **Knight**: a lattice walk of (±1, ±2). Its tours are a [Graph theory](/Mathematics/combinatorics/graph-theory.md) problem.
