# 🎲 Combinatorics

The mathematics of counting and arranging. Chess is its favourite playground.

## Classic chess problems

- **Eight queens**: place 8 queens so that none attacks another. There are 92 solutions, 12 up to symmetry ([Algebra](/Mathematics/algebra.md)). Gauss worked on it in 1850.
- **Knight's tour**: visit every square once with a knight → [Graph theory](/Mathematics/combinatorics/graph-theory.md).
- **Domination**: the minimum number of queens that attack every square is 5.

## How big is chess?

- About **4.8 × 10⁴⁴** legal positions.
- **10¹²⁰** possible games: the Shannon number (1950), a rough lower bound.
- About 10⁸⁰ atoms in the observable universe.

Chess is finite, but too large to search to the end. That is why [Chess engines](/Chess/engines.md) need heuristics and [Game theory](/Mathematics/game-theory.md) gives only an existence result.
