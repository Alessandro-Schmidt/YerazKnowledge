# 🕸️ Graph theory

Vertices joined by edges. Euler founded the field in 1736 with the bridges of Königsberg.

## The knight's graph

Let each square be a vertex and each knight move an edge. A **knight's tour** is then a Hamiltonian path; a closed tour is a Hamiltonian cycle.

- Euler studied tours in 1759.
- **Warnsdorff's rule** (1823): always move to the square that has the fewest onward moves. It is a greedy heuristic that almost always works.
- There are about 26 trillion closed tours on the 8×8 board.
