# ∞ Analysis

The mathematics of limits: calculus, continuity, series, and functions of real and complex variables.

## Sergey Mergelyan (1928–2008)

An Armenian mathematician from Yerevan. In 1951, at 20, he became the youngest Doctor of Sciences in the history of the USSR. His **Mergelyan's theorem** (1951) answers a classical question in complex approximation: when can a function be approximated uniformly by polynomials?

He is the modern heir of the line that begins with [Anania Shirakatsi](/Armenia/armenia/anania-shirakatsi.md) → [Armenia](/Armenia/armenia.md).
