# 📊 Probability & statistics

The mathematics of uncertainty, and of learning from data.

## The Elo rating system

Arpad Elo, a physics professor, designed it for chess; FIDE adopted it in 1970. The expected score of player A against player B is

E_A = 1 / (1 + 10^((R_B − R_A) / 400))

After each game, R_A changes by K × (actual score − E_A). A 400-point gap means about a 91% expected score.

Ratings sort the world's players: Levon Aronian reached **2830** in 2014 → [Chess in Armenia](/Chess/chess-in-armenia.md).
