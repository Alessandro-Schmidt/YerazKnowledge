# ➗ Algebra

The study of symbols and of the rules for combining them. It started with solving equations (al-Khwarizmi, 9th century) and grew into **abstract algebra**: groups, rings and fields.

## Groups

A group is a set with an operation that is associative and has an identity element and inverses. The symmetries of a square (4 rotations + 4 reflections) form the **dihedral group D₄**, of order 8.

- On a chessboard, D₄ explains why the [eight queens](/Mathematics/combinatorics.md) puzzle has 92 solutions but only 12 distinct ones: the others are rotations or reflections of them.
- In architecture, D₄ is the symmetry of [Etchmiadzin's](/Armenia/armenia/christianity/etchmiadzin.md) cross-in-square plan.
