# 🇦🇲 Armenia
<!-- yeraz {"color":{"heading":"amber"}} -->

A landlocked country in the South Caucasus. Capital: Yerevan. About 3 million people live there, and a larger diaspora lives abroad (Russia, the United States, France, Lebanon).

## Timeline

- **782 BC**: the Urartian fortress of Erebuni is founded. Yerevan counts its age from it.
- **95–55 BC**: Tigranes the Great; the kingdom stretches from the Caspian to the Mediterranean.
- **301 AD**: the first state to adopt Christianity → [Christianity in Armenia](/Armenia/armenia/christianity.md).
- **405**: [Mesrop Mashtots](/Armenia/armenia/christianity/mesrop-mashtots.md) creates the alphabet.
- **451**: Battle of Avarayr, a defence of the faith against Sasanian Persia.
- **7th century**: [Anania Shirakatsi](/Armenia/armenia/anania-shirakatsi.md) writes on arithmetic and astronomy.
- **1915–1923**: the Armenian Genocide in the Ottoman Empire.
- **1920–1991**: a Soviet republic; Yerevan becomes a scientific centre ([Sergey Mergelyan](/Mathematics/analysis.md)).
- **1991**: independence.
- **2011**: chess becomes a school subject → [Chess in Armenia](/Chess/chess-in-armenia.md).

## Identity

Three things hold Armenian identity together across the diaspora: the Church, the alphabet and Mount Ararat (now across the border in Türkiye). In the 20th century, chess joined them as a national passion.
