# ✝️ Khachkars

Carved stone slabs with a cross at the centre, surrounded by interlaced patterns. About 40,000 survive. In 2010 UNESCO added them to its list of Intangible Cultural Heritage.

The interlace is built from repeated motifs, rotations and reflections: a practical study of symmetry → [Geometry](/Mathematics/geometry.md).

Part of [Christianity in Armenia](/Armenia/armenia/christianity.md).
