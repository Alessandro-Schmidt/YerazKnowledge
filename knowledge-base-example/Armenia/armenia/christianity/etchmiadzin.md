# 🏛️ Etchmiadzin Cathedral

The mother church of the Armenian Apostolic Church, in Vagharshapat. Tradition dates it to 301–303. The current building is largely from 483–484, so it is among the oldest cathedrals in the world. It has been a UNESCO World Heritage Site since 2000.

## Its geometry

- A **cross-in-square** plan: four apses around a central dome.
- The dome sits on a drum: a circle carried by a square, by way of pendentives.
- The plan has the **4-fold symmetry** of a square (the dihedral group of order 8), the same group that acts on a chessboard → [Geometry](/Mathematics/geometry.md), [Algebra](/Mathematics/algebra.md).
