# ⛪ Christianity in Armenia
<!-- yeraz {"color":{"heading":"violet"}} -->

## Conversion

Tradition says King Tiridates III persecuted Gregory and held him in a pit at **Khor Virap** for 13 years. When Gregory was freed, he cured the king, who made Christianity the state religion in **301**. Gregory is honoured as *the Illuminator*. Khor Virap monastery, with Ararat behind it, is the country's best-known view.

## The Armenian Apostolic Church

- Led by the Catholicos of All Armenians from [Etchmiadzin](/Armenia/armenia/christianity/etchmiadzin.md).
- Did not accept the Council of Chalcedon (451). It belongs to the Oriental Orthodox family.
- Celebrates Christmas on **6 January**, together with Epiphany.

## Culture it produced

- The alphabet: [Mesrop Mashtots](/Armenia/armenia/christianity/mesrop-mashtots.md).
- Stone crosses: [Khachkars](/Armenia/armenia/christianity/khachkars.md).
- Monastery schools such as Tatev (14th century), which taught theology together with arithmetic and astronomy. That tradition produced scholars like [Anania Shirakatsi](/Armenia/armenia/anania-shirakatsi.md).
