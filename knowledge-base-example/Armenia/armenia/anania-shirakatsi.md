# 🧮 Anania Shirakatsi

A 7th-century Armenian scholar (c. 610–685), often called the father of the exact sciences in Armenia. He studied in Trebizond and came home to teach.

## Work

- **Questions and Solutions**: one of the oldest surviving collections of arithmetic word problems, with answers.
- Tables of addition, multiplication and division, written in [Mashtots' letter-numerals](/Armenia/armenia/christianity/mesrop-mashtots.md).
- Astronomy and calendars: he argued that the Earth is a sphere and explained eclipses.

## Why he matters here

He is the bridge between the Church's schools ([Christianity in Armenia](/Armenia/armenia/christianity.md)) and [Number theory](/Mathematics/algebra/number-theory.md) / [Geometry](/Mathematics/geometry.md). A line runs from him to [Sergey Mergelyan](/Mathematics/analysis.md) thirteen centuries later.
