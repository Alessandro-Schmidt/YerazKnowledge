# Code of conduct

Yeraz follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
In short:

## Our pledge

We want taking part in Yeraz to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, whatever their
age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality,
personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, and assume good intent.
- Respect different opinions and experiences.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and learn from them.
- Focus on what is best for the people who use Yeraz.

## What is not acceptable

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing someone else's private information without their explicit permission.
- Anything else that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in every project space (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) and when
someone represents the project elsewhere.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer,
[Alessandro Schmidt](https://github.com/Alessandro-Schmidt), at the email address on their
GitHub profile. Every report is reviewed and kept confidential.

The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other
contributions that do not follow this code. Depending on how serious it is, a breach may lead to
a warning, a temporary ban or a permanent ban from the project, as the Contributor Covenant's
[enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines)
describe.
